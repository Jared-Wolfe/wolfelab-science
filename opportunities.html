Opportunities

Training ecologists in the field and at the computer.

Students in the Wolfe Lab combine field natural history with quantitative tools to answer questions about wildlife populations in changing environments.

Current status

There are currently no funded MSc or PhD positions available.

This page will be updated when a funded graduate or seasonal field position opens. Prospective students with independent fellowship support or a specific external funding plan may still get in touch.

Research fit

Strong projects begin with a clear ecological question and a genuine connection to the lab’s ongoing work—not simply a general interest in wildlife.

Field ecology

Long days, difficult conditions, careful observation, and consistent data collection are central to much of our work.

Quantitative ecology

Students develop reproducible workflows and use demographic, spatial, acoustic, or other analytical approaches appropriate to their questions.

Conservation relevance

Projects should connect ecological understanding with the management or conservation of wildlife and their habitats.

Before you write

A concise, specific inquiry makes it much easier to understand your preparation and whether your interests align with the lab.

Read about the research

Identify the project or research theme that most closely matches the questions you want to pursue.

Explain the connection

Briefly describe the ecological questions that interest you and why the Wolfe Lab is a good setting for them.

Describe your preparation

Include relevant field, analytical, writing, coding, or collaborative experience. Attach a current CV.

Be clear about timing

State the degree sought, intended start term, and whether you have or are pursuing independent funding.

Graduate funding

Graduate positions are generally tied to grants, fellowships, or teaching support and cannot be offered without a confirmed funding source. An expression of interest is not an offer of admission or financial support.

I have worked with students to successfully secure several NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program and GEM fellowships. If you have a strong academic and research record and think you may be competitive for these or other external fellowships, please get in touch early so we can discuss potential research fit and application timing.

Any available lab-funded position will include a defined project, start date, and application process on this page.
